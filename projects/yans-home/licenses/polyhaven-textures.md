The following assets are distributed under [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license).

- [White Oak Veneer](https://polyhaven.com/a/white_oak_veneer), Jenelle van Heerden.
- [Wood Floor](https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_floor), Dimitrios Savva.

- [White Plaster 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/white_plaster_02), Rob Tuytel.

Local derivatives are resized/compressed to WebP and inherited from muko-home V11.
This draft uses the 12 standard colour/normal/roughness maps (four families).
The optional floor desktop files remain available but are not requested by this viewer.
Linen and plaster colour maps are neutralized for the scene palette. The stone
surface in `src/materials.ts` is procedural and is not a Poly Haven asset.
Source URLs and conversion details are in `public/materials/sources.json`.
Texture maps are self-hosted; the published site does not call Poly Haven.
